An overview of “If Found…”

⚠️ Warning: Mild spoilers ahead.

What

I first came across If Found… several years ago on Steam. It’s a game about identity, family. About coming out. It’s an experience, and a very important one to me at that.

It’s a game (okay… a “narrative experience”) made by a tiny Irish game studio called DREAMFEEL in 2020.

The experience

I remember I initially tried playing it and didn’t really enjoy it. I have then tried it years later without fully realising what I was getting myself into emotionally.

The game itself can be played at your own pace. You are basically reading Kasio’s diary for most of the game - she’s the protagonist. You erase every frame once you’ve read it to reveal new frames (the art is LITERALLY hand-drawn!).

There are two stories happening in parallel, the real Kasio’s story, and her space story where she imagines herself as an astronaut because of her fascination with space. In fact, her chosen name is based on the Cassiopeia constellation (Kasio -> Cassio-peia).

The music does absolute wonders for this game - 2 Mello did an absolutely fantastic job here. It can be insanely atmospheric, melancholic or energetic, nostalgic yet hopeful.

Music made me cry at the game quite a few times, and I’ve played it several times. Because the game isn’t voiced, the atmosphere it crafted purely through art, colours and music.

Why it matters to me

Kasio is a trans woman in her early 20s coming back home to Achill island from uni. She’s dealing with identity, recognition, relationships, emotion etc - in an early 90s rural Irish setting.

The last part of the game is about erasure and rebirth, which is symbolised by her burning her diary. As a trans person myself, who also keeps a diary I, and has struggled with people in my life, I can relate to her quite a bit.

It matters to me so much that I planned to go to Achill (London to Dublin then bus for HOURS) myself, not to see anything in particular but just to be there. Some people go to locations on famous events - I’m going to a location that made me cry.

DREAMFEEL have said that the game is a combination of fiction and developer’s personal experiences. Were Kasio a real person, I would just… hug her and tell her everything will be alright.

Closing thoughts

This isn’t a long game, perhaps only 3ish hours, depending on how quickly you can process a scene (and whether you have to stop to cry :D). I mean… it barely fits the definition of a game, it’s more of a narrative experience.

If you’re into narrative experiences, play it. Support a small, independent game studio.

So… it’s fair to say that If Found… is my favourite game.
